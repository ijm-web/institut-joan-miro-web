Erasmus+ programme

Erasmus+ is the European Union’s programme for education, training, youth and sport. In school education it funds student and teacher mobilities and cooperation projects between schools in different countries, to improve the quality of teaching, encourage language learning and strengthen the European dimension of schools.

Internationalisation and European projects

  1. 2012-2013First short-term mobilities
  2. 2023-2024Erasmus+ KA120 accreditation in school education
  3. TodayBilateral exchanges with schools in four European countries

Our school has experience in European projects and, since the 2023-2024 school year, holds the Erasmus+ KA120 accreditation in school education. This accreditation frames our internationalisation strategy and allows us to keep developing learning and cooperation opportunities with other European schools.

We promote the internationalisation of the school and have organised short-term mobilities since the 2012-2013 school year. We currently run bilateral exchanges with schools in the Netherlands, Germany, Italy and France.

As part of the Erasmus+ programme, we also promote a range of mobility and training activities, including:

  1. Teacher trainingAt educational institutions and specialist organisations in other countries.
  2. Expert visitsExperts and professionals from abroad visit our school.
  3. Job shadowingObservation periods that allow teachers to see other education systems, methodologies and ways of organising a school at first hand.

These experiences contribute to teachers’ professional development, encourage the exchange of good practice and enrich the school’s educational work.

The school’s Erasmus+ coordination team is made up of {{ centre.coordinacioErasmus | llistaNoms(lang) }}, who plan and follow up mobilities and European projects.

At the same time, they allow students to use languages in real contexts, discover other education systems and cultures, develop autonomy and responsibility, and grow as citizens open to the world.
