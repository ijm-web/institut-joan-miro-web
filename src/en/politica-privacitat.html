Legal informationPrivacy policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Institut Joan Miró processes personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD). This page explains what data is collected through this website, why, and what your rights are.

1. Data controller

  • Controller: Institut Joan Miró, a public school of the Department of Education and Vocational Training of the Government of Catalonia (Generalitat de Catalunya).
  • Tax ID (NIF): Q5855612G
  • Address: c. Verge de Montserrat, 53, 08940 Cornellà de Llobregat (Barcelona), Spain
  • Email: a8033900@xtec.cat
  • Data Protection Officer (DPO): DPO of the Department of Education and Vocational Training — dpd.educacio@gencat.cat

2. What data we process and why

Contact form

  • Data: name, email address and the content of the message.
  • Purpose: to answer your enquiry.
  • Legal basis: the consent of the person submitting the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Retention: for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry, unless the data must be kept to comply with a legal obligation.

Please do not send health data or other sensitive data through the form. For formal procedures (pre-enrolment, enrolment, absence notes), use the official channels of the school or the Department.

Browsing the website

The server hosting the website records technical connection data (IP address, date and time, browser) to keep it running and secure. This data is not used to identify anyone or to build profiles. For cookies, see the Cookie policy.

3. Photos and videos of students

The school only publishes photos and videos in which students can be identified if it has the explicit authorisation of their parents or legal guardians. This authorisation is normally given at enrolment.

  • Purpose: to share the school's educational activities on the website and on its official social media accounts (Instagram and YouTube).
  • Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Withdrawal: the authorisation can be withdrawn at any time by contacting the school office. Once it is withdrawn, the school will stop publishing new images and will remove those already published from the website within a reasonable time.
  • If you find an image published without authorisation, please let the school know and it will be removed immediately.

5. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. To do so, contact the school (by email or in person at the school office), stating which right you wish to exercise.

If you believe the processing does not comply with the law, you can contact the DPO or lodge a complaint with the Catalan Data Protection Authority (APDCAT).

6. Changes to this policy

The school may update this policy when the website's services or the law change. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
