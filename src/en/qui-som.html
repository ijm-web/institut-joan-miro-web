Our schoolWho we are

For over 30 years, Institut Joan Miró has accompanied students from Cornellà de Llobregat through secondary school and sixth form. We carry the name of one of Catalonia's great artists, and like him, we believe in forging our own path without ever losing our roots.

We are a school with a long-serving teaching staff — an average of around 25 years of teaching experience — working as a team, coordinated by form tutors, to give each student close, personal follow-up, both academically and personally.

We believe in effort and support. That's why we offer support classes and shape every activity so that everyone who leaves our classrooms does so with a solid academic and personal foundation.

Joan Miró, the painter

Our school carries the name of one of Catalonia's most universal artists. Like him, we share a wish to look at the world with curiosity and to grow while staying true to our own roots.
