Legal informationAccessibility statement

Commitment

Institut Joan Miró is committed to making its website accessible, in accordance with Royal Decree 1112/2018 of 7 September on the accessibility of public sector websites and mobile applications, which transposes Directive (EU) 2016/2102.

This accessibility statement applies to the institutjoanmiro.cat website. It does not apply to the school’s internal management areas (GESTIO and Programacions), which are restricted to staff.

Compliance status

This website is partially compliant with standard UNE-EN 301549:2022 (which includes the level AA success criteria of WCAG 2.1), due to the exceptions listed below.

Non-accessible content

Non-compliance: some published PDF documents (forms and information documents) may not be tagged so that they can be read correctly with screen readers.

Disproportionate burden: no disproportionate burden is claimed.

Content outside the scope of the applicable legislation: third-party content linked from the website (Clickedu, Government of Catalonia and Department of Education websites, YouTube videos and social media), which is not managed by the school.

Preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared on 8 November 2026 through a self-assessment carried out by the school (review of heading structure, reading order, colour contrast, image alternative texts, keyboard use and mobile behaviour). It will be reviewed every school year.

Feedback and contact details

You can report any accessibility problem with this website, or request information in an accessible format, by email at a8033900@xtec.cat or by phone on +34 93 377 51 66. We will reply within 20 working days.

Enforcement procedure

If, after submitting a communication or a request for accessible information, the response has not been satisfactory, you may file a complaint with the Department of Education and Vocational Training of the Government of Catalonia, as provided for in article 13 of Royal Decree 1112/2018.
